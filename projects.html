Projects

Care uptake among young people with gambling problems (current)

I study what leads adolescents to seek care for gambling problems. I take a socio-ecological perspective and ask how individual, social, and wider environmental factors shape the pathway from experiencing gambling problems to taking up care. The project is part of our research on gambling among young people at the University of Amsterdam.

TikTok Shop and impulsive buying

A preregistered survey among Dutch adolescents on TikTok Shop use, persuasion knowledge, and impulsive buying intentions.

SENTENCES: cancer screening and misinformation

Social mEdia aNalysis To promotE caNCEr Screening. In this multidisciplinary project with linguists, communication scientists, data scientists, and AI researchers, we study how Dutch cancer screening programmes are discussed online and where misinformation enters those conversations. We combine news coverage from 2010 to 2022, a corpus of X/Twitter posts, and an online forum, using corpus analysis, hybrid thematic analysis, and agent-based modelling. The project is led by Prof. Enny Das at Radboud University.

Political microtargeting (PhD)

My doctoral work at the University of Duisburg-Essen tested whether disclosing that a political ad was targeted changes how people judge it. Studies covered disclosure labels and credibility, a calculus approach to perceived targeting, persuasion awareness and coping, and an eye-tracking experiment on visual attention to transparency cues.
